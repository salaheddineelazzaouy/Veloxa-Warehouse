Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 July 2026  ·  Veloxa SARL, Casablanca, Morocco

Terms & Conditions Privacy Policy Refund Policy

Your data belongs to you. Veloxa SARL processes personal data as a data processor under Moroccan Law 09-08 (CNDP). We never sell your data to third parties.

1. Who We Are

Veloxa SARL ("Veloxa", "we", "us") is a limited liability company incorporated under Moroccan law, with its registered office in Casablanca, Morocco. We operate the Veloxa Warehouse inventory management platform at veloxa.ma.

Data Protection contact: privacy@veloxa.ma

3. Legal Basis for Processing

We process personal data on the following legal bases under Law 09-08:

  • Contract performance — processing necessary to deliver the Service you subscribed to
  • Legal obligation — compliance with Moroccan tax and accounting laws
  • Legitimate interest — security monitoring, fraud prevention, service improvement
  • Consent — marketing communications (opt-in only; you may withdraw at any time)

4. How We Use Your Data

  • Providing, maintaining, and improving the Service
  • Sending transactional emails (invoices, password resets, subscription alerts)
  • Security monitoring and anomaly detection via our audit log system
  • Complying with legal obligations under Moroccan law
  • Sending product updates and news (only with your consent)

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including:

  • AES-256-GCM field-level encryption for sensitive PII (phone numbers, addresses)
  • TLS 1.3 for all data in transit
  • Transparent Data Encryption (TDE) for data at rest in PostgreSQL
  • JWT tokens with 30-minute access token lifetime and rotation
  • Immutable audit logs — every PII access and mutation is logged with user ID, IP, and timestamp
  • Regular backups and point-in-time recovery

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We share data only with:

  • Paddle — payment processing (they act as Merchant of Record; governed by their own privacy policy)
  • Cloud infrastructure providers — hosting and storage under data processing agreements
  • Legal authorities — when required by Moroccan law or valid court order

7. Data Retention

  • Account data: retained for the duration of your subscription plus 30 days post-termination
  • Audit logs: retained for 5 years as required by Moroccan accounting law
  • Marketing consent records: retained until you withdraw consent
  • Anonymized usage analytics: retained indefinitely

8. Your Rights (Law 09-08 / CNDP)

Under Moroccan Law 09-08, you have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of all personal data we hold about you
  • Rectification — correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Erasure — request deletion of your data (subject to legal retention obligations)
  • Anonymisation — after 5 years of inactivity, PII is irreversibly anonymised
  • Portability — export your data in JSON or CSV format via the dashboard
  • Objection — object to processing based on legitimate interest
  • Withdraw consent — unsubscribe from marketing at any time

To exercise any right, email privacy@veloxa.ma. We respond within 30 days.

9. Cookies

We use only essential session cookies required for authentication. We do not use third-party tracking cookies or advertising cookies. No cookie consent banner is shown because we only use strictly necessary cookies.

10. International Transfers

Your data is processed and stored within the European Economic Area (EEA) or Morocco. If data is transferred outside these regions, we ensure adequate protections via standard contractual clauses.

11. CNDP Registration

Veloxa SARL has notified the Commission Nationale de contrôle de la protection des Données à caractère Personnel (CNDP) of its data processing activities as required by Law 09-08.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. We will notify you by email at least 14 days before material changes take effect.

13. Contact & Complaints

Privacy questions: privacy@veloxa.ma
Address: Veloxa SARL, Casablanca, Morocco
You may also lodge a complaint with the CNDP at www.cndp.ma.
