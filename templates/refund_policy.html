Refund Policy

Last updated: 1 July 2026  ·  Veloxa SARL, Casablanca, Morocco

Terms & Conditions Privacy Policy Refund Policy

Short version: Free trial for 14 days — no credit card required. After that, we offer a 7-day money-back guarantee on your first paid billing period if the Service does not meet your reasonable expectations.

1. Free Trial

All new accounts receive a 14-day free trial with full access to Starter plan features. No payment method is required during the trial. If you do not subscribe at the end of the trial, your account will be suspended and data retained for 30 days before deletion.

2. Money-Back Guarantee

7-day money-back guarantee on your first paid billing period (monthly or annual). If you are not satisfied for any reason, contact us within 7 days of your first charge for a full refund.

To request a refund under this guarantee:

  • Email billing@veloxa.ma within 7 days of your first charge
  • Include your account email and order reference
  • No reason required — we will process the refund within 5–10 business days

3. When Refunds Are Granted

✓ Eligible

First billing period within 7 days — full refund, no questions asked

✓ Eligible

Service outage exceeding 72 consecutive hours — pro-rata credit for the affected period

✓ Eligible

Duplicate charge due to billing error — full refund of the duplicate amount

✗ Not eligible

Requests after 7 days from first charge on an ongoing subscription

✗ Not eligible

Partial month or partial year — subscriptions are non-refundable after the 7-day window

✗ Not eligible

Account terminated for violation of Terms & Conditions

4. Annual Subscriptions

Annual subscriptions are eligible for the 7-day money-back guarantee on the initial payment. After 7 days, annual subscriptions are non-refundable. You may cancel at any time to prevent auto-renewal; your access continues until the end of the paid period.

5. Cancellation

You may cancel your subscription at any time from your account settings or by emailing billing@veloxa.ma. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period. You retain full access until that date.

Important: Cancelling a subscription does not automatically trigger a refund. Refund eligibility is determined by the rules above.

6. Refund Processing

Approved refunds are processed through Paddle (our Merchant of Record) back to the original payment method. Processing time:

  • Credit/debit cards: 5–10 business days
  • Bank transfers: 7–14 business days

Veloxa SARL is not responsible for delays caused by your bank or card issuer.

7. Disputes

If you believe a charge is incorrect, please contact us at billing@veloxa.ma before initiating a chargeback with your bank. We resolve billing disputes within 5 business days and prefer to settle issues directly.

8. Contact

For all billing and refund inquiries:

  • Email: billing@veloxa.ma
  • Response time: within 1 business day
  • Address: Veloxa SARL, Casablanca, Morocco
